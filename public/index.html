        How Pathfinder works

        1. Tell it about you. Add your major, a few interests, and the courses you’ve taken, in the left-hand column or with the guided setup. Each one is optional.
        2. Browse suggestions. Pathfinder reads every course description in the official UM catalog and ranks the ones that match your interests and build on what you’ve taken.
        3. Save what you like. Click “Save to plan” on a course. Then check the other tabs: My degree, Minors & certificates and Skills & careers.

        Common questions

        Is this my official degree audit?
        No. Pathfinder is for exploring ideas. Your degree audit and your advisor have the final word on what counts toward your degree.
        Where is my information saved?
        Only in this web browser on this device. Nothing is sent to UM or anyone else. Use “Download my plan” to keep a copy or move it to another computer.
        What does “possible substitution” mean?
        A course from another department that looks a lot like one of your major’s electives. Some departments will count it toward your major if you ask. Show it to your advisor.
        What does “prerequisite needs a check” mean?
        The course asks for something Pathfinder can’t verify, like instructor consent or junior standing. Read the requirement, or ask the department.
        Where do I find my course codes?
        On your unofficial transcript or your degree audit. A code is a subject plus a number, like “WRIT 101” or “BIOB 160”.
        Who can help me decide?
        Your academic advisor, for degree plans and substitutions. UM’s Career Development office, for career questions and job searches.

                What do the colored labels mean?
                Green
                Good news: counts toward your program, or you meet the prerequisites.
                Blue
                Counts toward a minor or certificate you could add.
                Amber
                Worth asking your advisor about, such as a possible substitution or a prerequisite Pathfinder can’t check.
                Red
                You need a prerequisite first.
                Tan
                The Gen Ed requirement the course fills.

                Every label also says what it means in words, so you never have to rely on the color alone.
